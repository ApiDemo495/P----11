## Gate

A sleeve is `open` only when ALL hold, out of fold:

1. OOF Net EV > 0 bps (friction charged on every trade)
2. OOF profit factor > 1.25
3. strict majority of folds positive
4. correlation-adjusted |t| >= 2.0 (conservative of iid and block-bootstrap t)
5. locked holdout Net EV > 0 with at least 25 trades

Fill model: decision at bar `t` close, fill at bar `t+1` **open**, exit at bar
`t+H` close. Round-trip friction 12 bps (BTC/ETH/BNB/alts), 18 bps (PAXG).

### PAXG: not measurable, not falsified

PAXG's tape is 18 months (13,262 hourly bars, 2025-03-27 to 2026-09-30).
The purged walk-forward requires **30 closed training trades** before it
will evaluate a fold. PAXG's best entry-gated trend produces:

| fold start | closed trades available for training |
|---|---|
| 2025-06-07 | 1 |
| 2025-08-18 | 1 |
| 2025-10-28 | 2 |
| 2026-01-08 | 5 |

So no fold can be scored, `purged_select` returns no out-of-fold trades and
the family never reaches the gate. That is a **data-depth** verdict. It is
recorded separately from a negative result because the two imply different
actions: a falsified family should be abandoned, an unmeasurable one needs a
longer tape. PAXG therefore stays a hedge display, not an alpha asset.
